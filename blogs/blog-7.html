BlogTop 10 Ways to Enhance Candidate Engagement

For being competitive, companies need to transform their recruitment strategies to seamlessly engage, identify, and appoint a record high number of appropriate candidates.

Organization’s internal recruitment function ensures an opportunity to engage and hire the appropriate candidates. Most of the time, however, executing a perfect recruitment strategy proves to be complicated for recruiters. To un-complicate this, experts at Brainmeasures devised a comprehensive employee hiring model and named it as Appropriate Candidate Engagements (ACE).

ACE is an intelligent candidate recruitment strategy comprises technology solutions and hiring strategies to consistently engage appropriate candidates at the right time and with the right approach.

Propel the Recruitment Optimization Efforts with ACE

Through the ACE strategy, recruiters can design efficient and streamlined recruiting processes to drive improvements in conversion rates throughout the recruitment channel.

Discussed here below are the Top 10 Ways to implement the ACE strategy for recruiting processes for optimizing every stage of the hiring process:

Candidate-Centric Recruitment Strategy:

Recruiters have realized that employing administrator-centric strategies and just filling the job-requisitions is not doing any good to their employee hiring-projects. Recruiting managers, therefore, must opt for a candidate-centric strategy to effectively engage with appropriate caliber at the right time, with the right approach, throughout the entire recruitment process and post-appointment.

Enhance Candidates’ Engagement Experience:

Modern recruiting need to focus on an enhanced candidates’ engagement experience beginning from the first look at the employer’s career website to the first day at the work-site, and beyond.

Promotional Career Webpage

Correct brand-image promotion is the initial attempt to connect and interact with appropriate and competitive candidates. Employers must, therefore, strategically design their career webpage to ensure stellar engagement from the desired caliber.

Social Recruiting

Employers must harness the power of their existing workforce through social referral channels, like LinkedIn, and others. These referral initiatives will enable recruiters, not just post job-requirements directly on social media feeds of the current employees, but shall allow them to connect effectively with the desired candidates.

Advanced Automation

Employers must integrate ultra-modern technologies, such as Artificial Intelligence (AI) and machine learning, to automate tedious tasks throughout the recruitment process, including skill-test-proctoring. These smart technology integrations will enable recruiters to constructively spend more time on developing meaningful strategies and on-boarding appropriate candidates.

Agile Integrations

Employers are always hiring, and candidates are constantly searching. Recruiters, therefore, must act proactively throughout their recruitment process to ensure successful hiring of quality caliber. By embedding connected technologies, like text interviewing, chat-bots, and others, to the employee hiring protocol, recruiters can quickly engage and appoint the top talent for their organization.

Digitizing On-Boarding

Employers must focus on streamlining their employee on-boarding processes. They can plan to replace the painful administrative paper-work with digitized forms and prioritize the critical tasks that the new-hire can finish even before their first day at the work-site.

Fine-Tune the Job-Application Process

Brainmeasures research team revealed that nearly 55% of applicants drop a complicated online application process. Enabling fewer clicks in the online job-application interface shall ensure a large count of submissions from relevant applicants.

Passive Candidates’ Pool

Recruiters must additionally create a talent pool of passive candidates nurtured through social media and email marketing campaigns, which recruiters periodically launch for their employer’s brand promotion.

Real-Time Communication

Hiring teams shall closely track and monitor all their communications with the candidates to ensure a seamless flow of their recruitment program.

To Sum Up

Meticulously devised by the experts at Brainmeasures (a leading online training certifications and skill-assessment solutions provider), ACE strategy enables recruiters to improve the quality, speed, and effectiveness of candidates’ engagement throughout their hiring process that starts from candidates getting familiar with the employer’s brand till they are on-board, and further ahead.
